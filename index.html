Section 1
Coaching Philosophy

"If you try to stand out in everything, you will stand out in nothing."

① Player Development / Improvement
  • Players should measurably improve through practice & over the summer.
  • Practice will serve as an effective learning environment; specific, high-intensity reps for every individual.
  • We will spend time on general (i.e. shooting & finishing) & action specific skill development.
★ Goal = create better basketball players
② Competition / Effort / Toughness / Urgency
  • Every drill is competitive; we will keep track of score, winners get to run.
  • Run everywhere; everything is timed.
  • Sink or swim style — you figure it out or you don't.
★ Mentality = toughness, grit, & extra effort
③ Campo-style Half-court Defense Identity
  • Spend 30+ minutes of individual / team defense scheme & principles.
  • Clear expectations & vocalized emphasis of identity.
★ Emphasis = no strong-hand, ball pressure, close-outs, & help defense
